# 2026-08-20

## PoE 2 market history

PoE 2 now publishes a reusable current market catalogue plus
`price-history.json` for every tracked league. The history uses one shared
timestamp axis with aligned item-price and Divine-rate arrays, preserves missing
quotes as gaps, keeps 7 days hourly, then one sample per UTC day for up to 430
days. Scheduled runs merge the deployed timeline before appending; code-only
deployments reuse it unchanged.

Added a native PoE 2 Price tracker tab. A searchable left-hand browser groups
markets into currency, crafting, gems, unique gear, jewels/relics,
maps/tablets, fragments/keys, league items, and other, with nested item-family
filters inside each applicable group and the matching items beside the category
tree. Selected parents toggle their nested filters open or closed. Category
rules use the upstream market family plus GGG item class, tags, Metadata path,
inheritance path, and base type. The fetcher enriches every source through the
RePoE base-item export and keeps poe.ninja's exact unique-item category, so new
items are classified without display-name lists. Unique gear is split by
weapon, individual armour and accessory slots, offhands, flasks, and charms.
The checked challenge and Standard catalogues have no uncategorized items; the
challenge catalogue has no items in Other unique gear. Reliquary Keys contains
the 9 actual keys; pinnacle access items and components use Encounter Keys,
while Crisis items remain Fragments. It plots 24-hour, 7-day,
30-day, or full timelines, follows the global currency
choice, and shows current source plus listing or trade-volume context. A
Divine-adjusted option reports whether the item outpaced Divine using the rate
stored at both ends and overlays that rate on the chart. The PoE 2 overview
links to the tracker and reports how many markets are available.

## PoE 2 Currency Exchange

The official GGG adapter now retains the complete completed-pair graph instead
of only the selected Exalted price. Current and historical files preserve both
volumes, normalized traded bounds, stock bounds, item metadata, and the official
market hour. Independent poe.ninja quotes remain available after source merging.

Reworked the Currency Exchange tab around a route finder. For each item it now
normalizes every usable Exalted, Divine, Chaos, or other completed quote pair
through the same-hour Exalted market, recommends the cheapest buy or best sell
route, and guards both legs with a configurable turnover floor. Quantity results
show quote units, Exalted equivalent, observed low/high range, limiting turnover,
and an hourly-flow estimate. The market catalogue exposes the best currencies
and route disagreement per item; completed range charts and Divine-adjusted
history remain available below the finder.
Route listings render 10 rows initially and can be expanded in 10-row batches.
Available Exalted, Chaos, and Divine comparisons are pinned into the first
batch even below the turnover floor, while recommendations still respect it.
Changing the selected market, direction, or turnover filter resets the list.
Clicking any route adds a separate comparison card below the recommendation and
highlights the selected currency row without replacing the recommended route.
Replaced the fixed turnover and hourly-flow dropdowns with editable numeric
fields and liquid-market suggestions. Defaults are now 1,000 Exalted/hour and
90% of completed hourly flow. Planned quantity starts at one item instead of
2,000 and remains editable for bulk checks.
Moved Largest route disagreements below the route table into a compact
two-column panel so the comparison columns retain the router's full width.
Added a pinned-currency legend and row badges for Exalted, Chaos, and Divine;
a core currency that is also recommended visibly carries both states.
The bottom market table now defaults to 1,000 Exalted turnover, shows 20 rows,
and expands 20 at a time. Search, sort, and turnover changes reset the batch.
Sell mode is now the route finder's default. Pinned Exalted, Chaos, and Divine
rows remain guaranteed in the first batch but are placed at their actual
Exalted-equivalent rank among the displayed routes.
Extracted the Price Tracker item browser into a shared PoE 2 component and use
it in Exchange as well. The route finder now has category and subcategory counts,
name search, and an adjacent filtered item list instead of a flat item dropdown.
Both tabs keep the browser as a bounded, sticky desktop sidebar beside the
route finder or chart and stack it only on narrow screens. Category and item
panes scroll independently; long labels truncate instead of compressing list
rows. Collapsing a category also clears its selected subcategory instead of
leaving an invisible filter active.
