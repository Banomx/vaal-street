# 2026-08-29

## PoE 2 Popular farms

Each farm card now puts entry price, entry movement, output-basket movement,
return versus entry, and cleared turnover in one decision strip. The card also
labels whether outputs or entry costs are winning without presenting the
market-pressure index as profit per map.

Liquidity now uses poe.ninja's stored Exalted turnover when completed unit
volume is unavailable. Unit volume, turnover and stash listings keep distinct
labels; quotes with no depth evidence identify their source instead of showing
generic "Liquidity unknown" text.

The PoE 2 shell now uses the Ardura caravan loading-screen artwork as a darkened,
fixed background. The treatment stays scoped to PoE 2 and includes a visible
Grinding Gear Games credit at the bottom of the page.

## PoE 2 Exchange

The route finder now presents completed-hour routes as observed evidence rather
than live recommendations. It filters by both Exalted turnover and completed
units, grades confidence from depth, range width and route disagreement, defaults
the user's assumed share to 25%, and honours assumptions down to 0.1%. Full
market flow remains visible separately as units/hour and Exalted/hour.

The best-observed card splits confidence evidence into numeric tags, and every
listed route has its own High, Caution or Low confidence badge beside its name.

Route selection now drives the exact two-leg history chart. Turnover labels use
per-hour units throughout, the lower table is named as an opportunity scanner,
and the narrow layout collapses the market browser after a selection while
keeping a sticky change-item control.
